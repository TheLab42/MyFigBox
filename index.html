English

MyFigBox respects your privacy.

MyFigBox does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Information about your collection is stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud synchronization, this data may be synchronized between your devices through Apple’s iCloud service and your Apple Account.

Camera access is used solely for the code-scanning feature that identifies minifigures. Camera images are neither stored nor transmitted by MyFigBox.

MyFigBox does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

For privacy questions: lab42@tutamail.com
